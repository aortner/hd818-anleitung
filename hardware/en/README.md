# 2. GNSS Antenna

![](../../bilder/hw/gnss-position.png)

**Choosing the mounting position**

1. The antenna sits in the **center of the tractor** (on the longitudinal center line).
2. The antenna is **vertical** when the tractor stands on level ground.
3. The best position is **directly above the rear axle**.

![](../../bilder/hw/gnss-dach.jpg)

> **Please note:**
>
> - Peel the protective film off the double-sided tape under the antenna base so the antenna sticks firmly to the roof.
> - The antenna cable connector points **to the rear**.

## 2.1 Radio Antenna

The radio antenna is **only needed with your own radio base station**. If you receive corrections via the SIM card (NTRIP), it does not need to be installed.

1. Mount the radio antenna at least **50 cm** horizontally away from the GNSS antenna.
2. The antenna is vertical when the tractor stands on level ground.

![](../../bilder/hw/funkantenne.jpg)

> **Please note:** Peel the protective film off the tape under the antenna base so the antenna sticks firmly.

## 2.2 Vehicles Without Cab

On vehicles without a cab, the GNSS antenna is usually mounted on the **hood**, the **roll bar** or the **rear seat support** (areas marked in red).

![](../../bilder/hw/gnss-ohne-kabine.png)

# 3. Steering Motor

## 3.1 Removing the Original Steering Wheel

1. Pry off the steering wheel cover with a flat screwdriver.

   ![](../../bilder/hw/lenkrad-abdeckung.jpg)

2. Locate the lock nut.

   ![](../../bilder/hw/lenkrad-mutter.jpg)

3. Undo the nut and pull the steering wheel off the steering column.

   ![](../../bilder/hw/lenkrad-abnehmen.jpg)

> **Please note:** The steering wheel is often stuck and has to be knocked loose. Screw the nut back on until it is **flush with the end of the steering column** first, so the thread is not damaged.

## 3.2 Choosing the Flange

The right flange depends on the **steering column diameter** and the **keyway width or number of teeth** of the spline.

![](../../bilder/hw/flansch.png)

![Flange on the steering column](../../bilder/hw/flansch-montiert.jpg) ![Steering column spline](../../bilder/hw/lenksaeule-keilwelle.jpg)

> **Please note:**
>
> 1. Every vehicle needs the matching flange. A wrong flange damages the steering column spline.
> 2. In rare cases the spline sticks out above the flange. Then add washers until flange and spline are flush.

## 3.3 Choosing the Bracket

A bracket stops the steering motor from turning with the wheel. There are four versions:

![](../../bilder/hw/halterungen.png)

| Bracket | Note |
|----|----|
| No. 1 (standard, included) | Clamp inner diameter 45 mm, length 100 mm |
| No. 1 (long version) | Clamp inner diameter 45 mm, length 150 mm / 200 mm |
| No. 2 | Alternative if No. 1 does not fit |
| No. 3 (for rice transplanters) | Clamp inner diameter 30 mm, length 100 mm |

Order of spring washer, washer and buffer:

![](../../bilder/hw/schraube-reihenfolge.png)

> **Please note:**
>
> - Fit all screws with a **spring washer**; the motor bracket screws with a **spring washer and a flat washer**.
> - Observe the order of spring washer and flat washer (picture above).
> - With bracket No. 1, position the clamp so that the **steering column bearing** is not damaged.
> - With bracket No. 2, make sure bracket and housing do **not fit too tightly**.
> - Mount the bracket flat against its contact surface.

# 5. Terminal

Mount the terminal where the driver can **see and operate it easily** without being hindered while driving. It usually goes **front right** in the cab.

![](../../bilder/hw/terminal-montiert.jpg)

> **Please note:**
>
> - When screwing the RAM mount to a cab pillar or cross member, watch out for cables running inside.
> - If the RAM mount cannot be screwed on, use a **U-clamp** (picture).

![](../../bilder/hw/terminal-ram.jpg)

# 6. Gyro Steering Angle Sensor

The angle sensor is fastened with its bracket to a bolt that **turns with the front wheel** (e.g. on the steering knuckle).

![](../../bilder/hw/winkelsensor-1.jpg) ![](../../bilder/hw/winkelsensor-2.jpg)

![](../../bilder/hw/winkelsensor-kabel.jpg) ![](../../bilder/hw/winkelsensor-befestigung.jpg)

> **Please note:**
>
> - Tie the cable to the bracket so there is **no strain** on the connector.
> - Mount the sensor **horizontally**.
> - Leave the cable neither too long nor too short – it must not be stretched at full steering lock.
> - Check that the sensor **does not hit anything** through the full steering and suspension travel.

# 7. Camera

Mount the camera at the rear of the cab roof and aim it so the working area behind the tractor is fully visible. The camera image can be switched on and off on the terminal.

![](../../bilder/hw/kamera-dach.jpg) ![](../../bilder/hw/kamera-schalter.jpg)

# 8. Wiring Harness

![](../../bilder/hw/kabelplan.png)

> **Please note:**
>
> - Keep cables away from **hot parts** (engine, exhaust).
> - Route cables so they **do not hinder** the driver.
> - Do not route cables too tight and do not kink them.

# 9. Power Supply

Supply voltage: **9–30 V**.

![](../../bilder/hw/batterie.jpg)

- **One battery (12 V):** positive terminal to the red wire, negative terminal to the black wire.
- **Two batteries in series (24 V):** positive terminal of the battery connected to the starter to the red wire, negative terminal of the battery connected to ground (frame) to the black wire.
- **Two batteries in parallel (12 V):** positive and negative terminal of either battery to red and black.

> **Please note:**
>
> - With 24 V (two batteries in series), **do not tap just one battery**.
> - Connect **directly at the battery terminals**.
